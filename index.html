Project

Paper Trading Exchange

Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · React · Docker

A fake stock exchange with a real order book. You trade against four kinds of bots (value, momentum, noise, market makers), then hit the market with news or a flash crash and watch it react. Momentum traders made prices swing 6.7× more. Removing the market makers made trading 2.6× more expensive.
