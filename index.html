Research

How LLMs make decisions with biased information

UNC School of Data Science and Society

Can an AI model tell when the information it's given is biased? In our setup, an advisor who earns a commission shows the model one hand-picked hotel review at a time, and the model has to learn how much to trust it. I compare how AI models handle this against a standard statistics approach, using about 7,500 real hotel reviews.
